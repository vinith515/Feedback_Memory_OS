# The 90-Second Winning Demonstration Script

**Tagline:** *"Your customers change. Your feedback memory shouldn't."*

---

## ⏱️ Timeline & Narration Flow

### 0–15 Seconds: The Problem & The Thesis
* **Action:** Open Dashboard (`http://127.0.0.1:8000/`).
* **Script:**
  > "Most feedback tools tell product teams what customers said yesterday. They produce isolated summaries and forget context. When products change, conventional AI doesn't know what you previously tried or how different segments responded.
  >
  > Feedback Memory OS turns customer feedback into persistent organizational memory powered by Hindsight."

### 15–35 Seconds: "What Changed?" Historical Synthesizer
* **Action:** Click `"What Changed?"` in the sidebar & click `Synthesize Memory`.
* **Script:**
  > "Notice how our agent doesn't just retrieve text. It traces the full 7-month arc of our onboarding problem:
  > In January, customers found navigation confusing. In March, we launched Guided Onboarding. SMB complaints dropped by 85%. But in June, enterprise accounts began reporting acute friction around API keys and custom webhooks.
  >
  > The agent knows: the original problem is resolved; the active problem is segment-specific enterprise API integration."

### 35–55 Seconds: Closed-Loop Decision Memory ("Did Our Fix Work?")
* **Action:** Click `Decision Memory`. Select `Launch Guided Onboarding V1` & click `Evaluate Outcome`.
* **Script:**
  > "Here is Decision Memory. We record our product decisions directly into Hindsight.
  > When we ask 'Did our fix work?', the agent compares historical memory before and after the decision.
  >
  > It reports: Before, 35 complaints (8 enterprise). After, 69 complaints (35 enterprise).
  > Verdict: Partial Success. It solved UI setup for SMBs, but unmasked an underlying enterprise API friction."

### 55–75 Seconds: The Definitive Ablation (Memory vs No-Memory)
* **Action:** Click `Memory vs No-Memory`. Click `Run Comparison`.
* **Script:**
  > "This is the core test. On the left: Stateless AI with only recent comments. It gives generic advice: 'Improve documentation and simplify setup.'
  >
  > On the right: Our Hindsight-Powered Agent. It knows we ALREADY launched Guided Onboarding and an API Wizard, that SMBs are thrilled, and that the remaining friction is strictly Enterprise SSO and webhooks.
  >
  > That is an 83% delta in historical accuracy."

### 75–90 Seconds: Conclusion
* **Action:** Click `🧠 Hindsight Memory Active` pill to open Memory Inspector.
* **Script:**
  > "Behind every claim are verified Hindsight memories, consolidated observations, and entity links.
  >
  > This isn't an AI that remembers a chat. It is an AI that remembers what your business has learned from its customers."
